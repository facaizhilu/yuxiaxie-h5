# Login background photo sources

The live login page uses a real photograph from Wikimedia Commons. It is cropped and slightly darkened, then exported as WebP; it is never upscaled.

## Current live background (`online/img/login-bg.webp`)

- Subject: Pha That Luang (That Luang), Vientiane, Laos, at dusk / sunset, showing the full stupa, surrounding palms and the plaza.
- File: [Naplemente Sztúpa.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Naplemente_Szt%C3%BApa.JPG)
- Author: [Belda10](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Belda10) (own work)
- Date: 10 November 2008 (camera); published on Commons 18 November 2015
- Original size: 4,272 × 2,848
- Used crop: a 2,221 × 2,848 central slice (full stupa and palms; 2,221 px wide natively, then downscaled)
- Export: 1,290 × 2,796 WebP, quality 88, slightly darkened. Extra height is filled with the photo’s dusk sky (top, 40 px) and a short dark fade under the plaza (bottom). No upscaling.
- Licence: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- Credit on the login page: “Pha That Luang · Belda10 · CC BY-SA 4.0”, linking to the Commons file.

A higher-resolution night photograph of the full stupa could not be found on Wikimedia Commons (the previous night photo, [PhaThatLuang Afterdark.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PhaThatLuang_Afterdark.jpg), is only 1,854 × 2,214 and looked soft on iPhone). This dusk photo was used instead, darkened slightly.
